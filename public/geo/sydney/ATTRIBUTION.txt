Sydney specialty pack (Greater Sydney LGA cluster)

Local Government Areas clipped from the national Australia pack LGA slice
(public/geo/australia/lgas/nsw.geojson), membership locked to ABS Greater
Sydney GCCSA-equivalent LGAs for Jetlag (33 LGAs including Strathfield and
City of Sydney).

Upstream: Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS), Australian Statistical
Geography Standard (ASGS) Edition 3 / ASGS2025 LGA, CC BY 4.0.
See public/geo/australia/ATTRIBUTION.txt for STE/LGA download pins and
simplification tolerances.

Coastline / sea_level_seed / international_border / POI: stubs
(source "none") until metro fill; bbox matches Greater Sydney reference AABB.

GTFS static bundle (public/geo/gtfs/sydney.json): Mobility Database mdb-2449
(Transport for NSW Greater Sydney complete static), clipped to the Sydney
specialty pack AABB. Attribution: Transport for NSW (CC BY 4.0). Rebuild:
node scripts/rebuild-all-gtfs-metros.mjs --metro sydney

Suburb and Locality (SAL) — public/geo/sydney/sals.geojson
Upstream: Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS), ASGS Edition 3 / ASGS2021 SAL,
CC BY 4.0. Source REST:
https://geo.abs.gov.au/arcgis/rest/services/ASGS2021/SAL/MapServer/0/query
Download: envelope intersects Greater Sydney reference AABB (west 150.0148,
south -34.3374, east 151.343, north -32.9961), NSW state filter, outSR=4326.
Server-generalized with maxAllowableOffset≈0.0014°, then @turf/simplify
tolerance 0.002 (same band as national Australia pack). Metro clip: keep SAL
features whose centroid lies inside one of the 33 Greater Sydney LGAs in
public/geo/sydney/lgas.geojson; parent lgaId is that containing LGA. Rebuild:
node scripts/geo/build-sydney-sals.mjs
